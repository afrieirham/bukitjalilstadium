# Bukit Jalil Stadium

A crowd-sourced reference of real seat views inside a single stadium, so fans can see what a seat actually looks like before buying a ticket.

## Language

**Stadium**:
The single physical venue this site documents: Bukit Jalil National Stadium, Kuala Lumpur. There is exactly one.
_Avoid_: Venue, arena, auditorium

**Section**:
A numbered seating block inside the Stadium (for example `201A-B`). The unit a seat view belongs to and the unit a page is built around.
_Avoid_: Sector, zone, area

**Contribution**:
A submission from a fan attached to a Section, carrying one or more photos and the seat details the fan supplies. A Contribution is private until it is reviewed.
_Avoid_: Upload, submission, entry

**Contributor**:
The fan who makes a Contribution, identified by a self-supplied name and an optional link. Contributors have no accounts.
_Avoid_: User, account, member
